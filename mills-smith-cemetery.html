Cemetery Details

Mills-Smith Cemetery

Location & Directions

From Boonville, go North on Fourth Street which becomes Folsomville Road. Turn right onto Phillips Road. Turn right onto Smith Road. Look for Mills Cemetery Road shortly after turning onto Smith Road. Follow the gravel and grass road up to the Cemetery.
