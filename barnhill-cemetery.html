Cemetery Details

Barnhill Cemetery

Location & Directions

Located in Skelton Township on Barnhill Cemetery Road. From Boonville, go north on Folsomville Road. Turn east on Phillips Road. The cemetery utilizes a shared driveway and is tucked behind a private home.
