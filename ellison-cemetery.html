Cemetery Details

Ellison Cemetery

Location & Directions

From Boonville, go North on Fourth Street which becomes Folsomville Road. Turn right onto Shelton Road until it comes to a T. Turn right onto Johnson Road. The cemetery is on the left on top of a hill. There is a white fence along the property line of the house next to the cemetery.
